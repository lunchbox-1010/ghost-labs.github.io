Refund and Cancellation Policy

How canceling and refunds work for Ghost Labs' subscriptions, software and services.

End User License Agreement Privacy Policy Refund and Cancellation Policy

Effective date: October 7, 2026 · Last updated: October 7, 2026

This policy covers purchases from Ghost Labs, LLC. Where a product has its own refund policy, that one applies to it: see Ravenwood's refund and cancellation policy.

1. Subscriptions

  • Cancel any time. Cancel from the product's account or billing page, or by writing to us. You keep access to the end of the period you've paid for, and you won't be charged again.
  • No refunds for part of a period. We don't refund the unused part of a month or year after you cancel.
  • Plan changes. Moving to a bigger plan charges the difference for the rest of the period; moving to a smaller one leaves a credit toward future invoices.

2. Apps and software

  • App Store and Google Play purchases are refunded by Apple or Google under their policies. We can't refund them ourselves.
  • Software bought directly from us: if it doesn't work as described and we can't fix it, contact us within 30 days of purchase and we'll refund it.

3. Professional services

Refunds for Enterprise Foundry and Brand Foundry work follow the signed agreement or statement of work for the engagement. Where it says nothing, deposits and fees for work already done aren't refundable, and we'll refund prepaid fees for work we haven't started.

4. Billing mistakes

If you were charged twice, charged after canceling, or charged the wrong amount, contact us and we'll refund the mistake in full. Where the law gives you a right to a refund, we'll honor it.

5. How to ask

Write to contact@ghost-labs.com with what you bought, the date and the email address you used. We reply within two business days, and approved refunds go back to the original payment method.
