Privacy Policy

What Ghost Labs collects through its website, when you contact us, and when you buy from us.

This policy explains what Ghost Labs, LLC ("Ghost Labs," "we," "us," or "our") collects through ghost-labs.com, when you contact us, and when you buy from us. Our products have their own privacy policies, which apply to information inside those products:

  • Ravenwood: Terms, Privacy and Refunds at ravenwood.app.
  • Tidy Task & Loot: Terms and Privacy.

The short version

This website has no analytics, no tracking and no advertising, and it sets no cookies. We don't sell personal information. We collect only what we need to answer you, sell to you, and support what you bought.

1. What we collect

  • When you contact us. Your name, email address or phone number, and what you write, so we can reply and follow up.
  • When you buy. Your name, email address, billing details and what you bought. Card payments are processed by Stripe; we receive the payment's status, amount and the card's last four digits, never the full number. App Store and Google Play purchases are handled by Apple or Google, who share only what they need to with us.
  • Client work. Information you give us during a services engagement, handled as your agreement with us says.
  • Visiting this site. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, processes your IP address and browser details to deliver pages and protect the service. We don't receive or keep those logs.

2. How we use it

To answer questions and provide support; to take payment, send receipts and invoices, and keep the records tax law requires; to deliver and license what you bought; and to keep our products secure. We email you about your purchases and accounts, not marketing, unless you ask for it.

3. Who else handles it

  • Stripe processes card payments.
  • Apple and Google handle purchases in their stores.
  • GitHub hosts this website.
  • Our email and hosting providers carry our messages and run our products.

We may also disclose information when the law requires it, to protect people or our services from harm, or as part of a sale or merger of the business, in which case this policy continues to apply.

4. How long we keep it

Correspondence is kept as long as it's useful for supporting you. Purchase and invoice records are kept as long as tax and accounting law requires. Ask us to delete anything else and we will.

5. Your rights

You can ask us to tell you what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, by writing to contact@ghost-labs.com. Depending on where you live (for example California, or the European Economic Area and the UK), you may have further rights, including to object to or restrict some processing and to complain to your data protection authority. We'll answer within the time the law sets.

6. Security

We use encryption in transit, limited access and reputable providers to protect your information. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your information, we'll tell you as the law requires.

7. Changes

We'll post any change here and update the date at the top.
